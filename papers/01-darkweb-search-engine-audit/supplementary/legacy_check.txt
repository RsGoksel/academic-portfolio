This figure is the empirical basis for the statement in §1 of the accompanying
manuscript that, of the six engines evaluated by Faizan and Khan (2019), only
two still resolve through a current Tor client. Replication: run the script in
this bundle (`legacy_check.py`, copy of the in-paper probe script) against a
fresh Tor circuit. Date-dependent results are expected; engine churn is the
phenomenon under measurement.

Notes
-----

* "Resolves" here means HTTP-200 within the timeout. A descriptor that loads
  but whose service refuses HTTP (Torch, Haystak) is operationally equivalent
  to an offline engine for a downstream researcher who wants to fetch SERPs.

* The status of Torch is genuinely ambiguous at the time of probe; it has
  alternated between operational and offline several times in 2024–2025
  according to public Twitter notices from its purported maintainers.

* This probe is the minimal reproducibility evidence for the §1 claim. It is
  not a full historical audit of the dark-web search-engine population and
  should not be treated as one.
