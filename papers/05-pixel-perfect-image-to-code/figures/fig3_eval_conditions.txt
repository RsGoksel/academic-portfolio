Primary statistical comparisons (pre-registered):
  H1: C5 beats C1 on human preference         (paired binomial, alpha = 0.05)
  H2: C6 beats C5 on SSIM                     (paired t-test on per-page SSIM)
  H3: C8 beats C7 on UI-polish human preference
  H4: C8 is the lowest cost-per-quality-unit among C4-C6 and C8
  H5: Iteration depth has diminishing returns; C5 - C4 < C4 - C1 on SSIM
