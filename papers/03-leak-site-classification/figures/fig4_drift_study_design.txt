Drift Study Window Design
=========================

Calendar time
|----2020----|----2021----|----2022----|----2023----|----2024----|----2025----|
              |            |            |            |            |
              v            v            v            v            v
           [W20]        [W21]        [W22]        [W23]        [W24..25]
           ~200          ~200         ~200         ~200         ~1000
           posts         posts        posts        posts        posts
                                                                ^
                                                                | training/
                                                                | calibration
                                                                | corpus
                                                                | (annotated)

  W20..W23: held-out historical windows. No annotation up-front.
            Apply best model frozen on W24..25 corpus.
            Stratified subsample of 50 posts/window receives full
            two-annotator labeling for ground-truth-on-historical.

  Reported metrics per window:
    - per-field accuracy vs subsample ground truth
    - macro-F1 industry
    - "Unknown" rate per field (proxy for content drift)
    - distribution of groups represented

  Hypothesis: per-group post conventions stabilize after ~30 posts;
  drift in per-field accuracy correlates with new group emergence,
  not with calendar time directly.

  Operational caveat: groups born after the calibration window
  (W24..25) cannot be evaluated retrospectively. Drift study is
  asymmetric: future-only.
